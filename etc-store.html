Meet the shop

Etc.Store brings together homewares, lifestyle objects and gifts in Copeland Park. Founded in 2023 by Jana and Marko as an extension of The Completist, it pairs contemporary design with vintage finds.

The shop’s curved displays and bright colours frame an assortment of objects for everyday use. Its own introduction puts the emphasis on the design and character of the pieces it selects.

Working here, shopping nearby

In the recorded conversation, a member of staff recalls visiting Traid to find second-hand shoes for a wedding. She also talks about being recognised at nearby places she visits, and about bringing friends to Copeland Park’s vintage shops and food market.

These answers connect a working day in Copeland Park with the surrounding neighbourhood: one shop worker is also a customer elsewhere.

Part of Bussey Alley

Etc.Store’s official visiting page places it in Studio BGC+D on Bussey Alley, opposite its sister shop, The Completist. These neighbouring spaces are part of Copeland Park’s mix of shops and creative businesses.
