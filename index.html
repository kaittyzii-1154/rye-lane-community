RYE LANE — LOOK AGAIN

Take a closer look at the street you pass through every day.

Centered on Rye Lane, this website brings together drawings, interactive experiences, and street interviews to invite people to rediscover details of everyday life that often go unnoticed. Building upon the street’s existing multicultural character, commercial activity, and community life, it aims to create a platform where residents, business owners, and visitors can observe, share, and engage with one another—allowing a diverse range of experiences and voices to collectively enrich the understanding of this street.
