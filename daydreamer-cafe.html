COFFEE & EVERYDAY ENCOUNTERS / RYE LANE

Daydreamer café

A coffee, a conversation, and a chance to get to know someone.

Different ways to stop

Some customers are on their way to the train. They pick up a coffee and carry on. Others stay and talk while their drinks are being made.

In this interview, a café worker describes these different rhythms of the day: the quick visit, the quieter afternoon, and the customer who comes in for a chat.

From a coffee to a conversation

One customer mentioned a show they were doing that evening. A brief conversation followed, and they exchanged names because the customer might come back.

It is a small encounter, but it offers a way to understand the café through the people who use it and the conversations that happen around its daily work.
